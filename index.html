STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Questions you're probably asking

Does this replace our EBR, eQMS, or MES?+
No. We connect to what you already run and assemble the evidence for review. We don't replace your platform, and the release decision stays with your QA team — always.
We're still on paper and Excel — is that a problem?+
No — that's exactly who we built this for. If your review still runs through manual logs or spreadsheets, we remove the chasing. We don't add another system for your team to learn.
How long does a pilot take?+
We scope every pilot to one plant and one agreed workflow. That's small enough to prove real results — review-prep time, outstanding-record age — before you commit to anything wider.
What does it cost?+
It depends on your scope — sources, checks, plant complexity. We'll tell you a real number on the call, after we understand your workflow, not before.
What happens to our data?+
We use it only to confirm fit and follow up about a pilot. We never sell or share it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.
